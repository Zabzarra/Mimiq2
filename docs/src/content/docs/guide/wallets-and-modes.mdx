A **wallet** in Mimiq is your profile for trading. It has its own mode, its own trader, its own settings and the venues you linked to it. Create more with `➕ New Wallet`, and pick the one you want in the menu.

## Two modes

| | Copy trading | Delta-neutral |
|---|---|---|
| What it does | Mirrors a trader's positions | Long on one venue, short the same token on another |
| You choose | A trader and the sizing | A pair, or a Smart DN plan |
| Needs | 1 venue | 2 venues |

**One mode runs per wallet.** To use both, use two wallets.

## Switching

Use `🔁 Switch to DN` or `🔁 Switch to Copy`. The switch takes effect within about 30 seconds.

<Aside type="caution" title="Before you switch">
The copy engine stops managing the wallet. Close or manage open copy positions yourself first, because they are not watched in delta-neutral mode. The same applies the other way round.
</Aside>
