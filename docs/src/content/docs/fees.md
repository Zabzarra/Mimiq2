Using Mimiq costs **1 bp (0.01%) of trade value** on the venues marked below. The venue collects it on top of its own fee.

**Lighter and Lighter RH:** Mimiq needs a Premium or Plus account. A Standard account is switched to Premium when you link it, so the Premium fees apply.
