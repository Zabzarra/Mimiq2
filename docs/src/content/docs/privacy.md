---
title: Privacy
description: What Mimiq stores about you.
---

**API credentials.** The keys you link are stored encrypted. The message with your key is deleted from the chat.

**Waitlist.** If you join, Mimiq stores your Telegram ID and name, and three answers: your interest, your venues and your monthly volume. No wallet address. No e-mail. Send `/leavewaitlist` in the bot to delete your entry.

**Your accounts.** Mimiq reads positions and orders on the venues you link. It needs this to trade for you.

**What Mimiq does not do.** It does not ask for your wallet's private key. It does not hold your funds.

**Third parties.** Telegram carries your messages. The venues see your orders.

Questions about your data: mimiq.tech@proton.me
