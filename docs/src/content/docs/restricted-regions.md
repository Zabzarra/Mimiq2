title: Restricted regions
description: Where Mimiq may not be used.

Mimiq works through trading venues. If a venue does not allow you to trade, you cannot use Mimiq with it. You are responsible for knowing and following the rules of the venues you link and of your own country.

## Not allowed on Mimiq

- The **United States**, the **United Kingdom** and **Canada**.
- **Countries and regions under comprehensive sanctions**: Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, and the Crimea, Donetsk and Luhansk regions of Ukraine.
- **People on a sanctions list** (including the US OFAC Specially Designated Nationals list and the UN, EU and UK sanctions lists), or owned or controlled by such a person.
- Anyone who uses a VPN or any other means to hide their location or get around a restriction.

As of 12 October 2026. Sanctions lists change; this page does not replace your own check.
