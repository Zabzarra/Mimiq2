## Stats

`📊 Stats` (or `/stats`) shows, for the selected wallet:

- Total volume
- Total PnL
- Total fees spent and the blended cost per 1M traded
- DN funding collected
- Volume, cost per 1M and fees for each venue

A leading `≈` means the fee figure is partly an estimate. `🔄 Refresh` reloads the numbers.

## History

`/history` lists your recent trades, and the closed delta-neutral pairs with their result.

## Status

`/status` shows the wallet, its mode and its trader.
