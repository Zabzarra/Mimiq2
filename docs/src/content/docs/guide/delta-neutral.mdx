A delta-neutral **pair** is a long on one venue and a short on the same token on another. You hold no price direction and collect the funding difference between the two venues. You need two linked venues.

Switch the wallet with `🔁 Switch to DN`, or open `/deltaneutral`.

## Open a pair

<Steps>

1. Tap `➕ New pair`.
2. Pick the long venue and the short venue, or a top-APR symbol from the list. HIP-3 markets on Hyperliquid are available through the `HIP-3` button.
3. Enter the leverage, or tap `⚡ Max Leverage`.
4. Enter the size. The screen shows the free margin on both venues.
5. Choose the entry: `⚡ Taker — 1x` (one market order per leg) or `📊 Taker — TWAP` (several timed tranches).
6. Optionally set stops and the funding auto-close, then `🚀 Open pair`.

</Steps>

## Manage a pair

`📊 Open pairs` lists your pairs. Open one to see its details and use:

| Button | Effect |
|---|---|
| `🎯 TP/SL` | Take-profit and stop-loss on both legs, as a price, a % move per leg, or a $ PnL per leg |
| `🛡 Auto-close` | Close the pair when funding turns against it |
| `💰 Cost cap` | Limit what the funding-turn close may cost; `🗑 Remove cap` clears it |
| `❌ Close` | Close both legs after a confirmation that shows the expected PnL |

<Aside type="caution">
Every pair is a real position on both venues. If one venue is unreachable, the bot tells you instead of guessing the state.
</Aside>
